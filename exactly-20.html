How it plays

Twenty dollars means $20.00.

Every customer names a price. Every station finds a new way to make that price hard to hit.

  1. 01

    Hold

    Squeeze the gold handle and the readout starts rolling.

  2. 02

    Ease off

    Feather the flow as the total creeps toward the order.

  3. 03

    Let go

    Land it on the cent for the best tip. Miss the order and you take a strike.

Career

Earn your place on the road.

Between shifts, the station is home base. Your map, your upgrades, your regulars and your trophies all live behind the counter.

Behind the counter

Walk out the door for the next shift. Upgrades you buy show up on the forecourt, keepsakes fill the cubbies, and achievements land in the trophy case.

  1. 01 · Shift results

    Three stars a shift.

    Clear the shift. Keep your strikes at zero. Land one exact pour. Six stars and the boss’s sign-off earn your promotion.

  2. 02 · Road map

    Twenty-one stops down the road.

    Every promotion lights the next stop on the map. Go back and master a station to certify it for bigger tips and a boss rematch.

  3. 03 · Garage

    Spend your tips.

    Steady Grip for a finer trickle. Turbo Nozzle for faster flow and bigger pay. Fuzzy Dice to forgive your first strike.

  4. 04 · Licence

    Level up your Licence.

    Every shift earns Licence XP. Levels unlock new pump handles and harder modifiers, like Tight Tolerance: half the margin, a bigger multiplier.

  5. 05 · Logbook

    Twenty-nine regulars.

    They come back with new orders and new chapters. Learn their usual, earn a keepsake, and keep your best pour on their page.

  6. 06 · Stamp Catalogue

    Make the station yours.

    Exact pours, streaks and boss sign-offs earn trading Stamps. Spend them on string lights, a new readout or a fresh finish for the pump.

Stations

What are they putting in the tank?

Every stop changes the pump. Twenty-one stations, each with its own fuel, rules and boss.

  • Gas-N-Go

    Regular unleaded

    Brenda usually wants twenty. Today her daughter is learning to drive, so it’s twenty-five.

  • Ye Olde Fill-Up

    Dragon’s Breath

    The fuel surges and the gauge burns. A dragon does not ask twice.

  • Runway 9

    Jet-A

    The tower calls a hold mid-pour. Rest the handle until you’re cleared.

  • Lucky Sevens

    Jackpot Juice

    The price per pull is hidden. Forget the price and watch the total.

  • Neon Megaplex 2099

    Plasma

    A self-driving cab wants $22.29 of plasma. Ping it when it’s done.

Modes

Pack for a road trip.

Reach the Big Rig Truck Stop and the road opens up. Pick a perk before the first leg, then stack more as you drive.

Road Trip

Six legs on a seeded route. Pick a perk before you set off and build on it as you drive. Share the seed so a friend can take the same trip.

Daily Trip

One shared route a day, one scored attempt. Come back tomorrow to keep the streak.

Endless Night Shift

Serve car after car on three strikes. An exact pour earns a strike back.

Weekly Challenge

A new rule card every week. Chase bronze, silver or gold; your best run counts.

After the finale

Round again?

Win the final boss’s sign-off at The Void Pump to open Overtime: each cleared shift climbs a rung, and one lost shift drops you back to the start.

New Game+ restarts the career with remixed fuels and a second twist for every boss. Your achievements, Stamps and looks come with you.
