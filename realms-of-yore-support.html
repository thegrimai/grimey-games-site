Continuing a campaign

Choose Continue on the title screen to reopen your saved campaign. If something looks different from your last session, note your build version and what changed.

Reading and motion

Text size, sound and reduced motion are in the game’s settings and saved on your device.

Saves

Clearing the app’s storage removes the campaigns saved there. Before switching builds, follow the save instructions that came with the build. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Reporting a problem

Email hello@grimey.games, or reply through the channel that sent your test build. Tell us the version, your device, where you were in the campaign and what you did. Check screenshots and campaign files for personal information before you send them.
