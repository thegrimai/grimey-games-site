Download the launcher

Open the download link in your itch.io purchase email to get the Grimea Alpha Launcher for Windows. If you’ve lost the email, find your itch.io purchase to get back to your download.

Update the alpha

Open the Grimea launcher and choose Update when a new version is available. When the update finishes, choose Play.

Use an earlier save

An alpha update can change which saves the game can read. If a character is missing or a save won’t open after an update, tell us which game version you last played and what happens when you try to load it.

Report a problem

Email hello@grimey.games. Include your Windows version, the launcher and game versions if available, and what you did just before the problem. For an update error, the exact message helps. For a gameplay issue, tell us which room or fight you were in.

Privacy questions

The studio privacy policy explains how we handle support messages. For the storefront and checkout, see itch.io’s privacy policy.
