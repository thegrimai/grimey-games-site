Who we are

Realms of Yore is made by Grimey Games, a studio of GrimAI, Inc. Questions about this policy or your data: hello@grimey.games.

Campaigns and settings

Your campaigns, including each generated world and settlement, are saved in the app’s storage. The game also remembers your settings, such as text size, sound, reduced motion and map orientation.

Offline by design

The game asks for no Android permissions and needs no account. It has no advertising, analytics or gameplay uploads, and we never receive your campaigns.

Backups

The app opts out of Android backup, so your campaigns aren’t copied to your Google account. Phone-to-phone transfer varies by device. If you export a campaign, keep that copy somewhere you trust.

Achievements and diagnostics

The game doesn’t report achievements to Google Play Games or send crash or diagnostic data. If that changes in a future version, we’ll update this policy first. If you send us a screenshot, save or report, we receive what you send.

Clearing your data

Clearing the app’s storage removes your campaigns and settings. Exported copies and files you’ve shared are separate. Questions? Email hello@grimey.games.
