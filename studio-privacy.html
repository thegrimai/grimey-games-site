Who we are

Grimey Games is a label of GrimAI, Inc. Questions about this policy or your data: hello@grimey.games.

What this site collects

Nothing beyond what any website receives. There is no advertising, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party embeds. Pages, images and fonts all come from this site.

Server requests

When you load a page, your browser sends a standard request to the server that hosts the site, including your IP address and browser type. Links to other websites are covered by those sites’ own policies.

When you contact us

If you email hello@grimey.games or reply to us about a test build, we receive your contact details, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and look into the issue.

Your information

To see, correct or delete something you sent us, email hello@grimey.games or reply in the same conversation. For details about an Android game, see its privacy policy below.
