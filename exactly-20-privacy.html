Exactly $20Privacy Policy

Exactly $20 doesn’t collect, sell or send any personal or device data to us. It has no ads, no analytics and no account of its own, and it plays offline.

Who we are

Exactly $20 for Android (package games.grimey.exactly20) is made by Grimey Games, a label of GrimAI, Inc. Questions about this policy or your data: hello@grimey.games.

What the game stores

On your device, in the app’s private storage: your progress (tips, stations, upgrades, save slots and any trip in progress), your profile (Licence XP, achievements, lifetime stats, Stamps, cosmetics and the logbook) and your settings (volume, haptics, layout and colours).

Backups

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include your saves in your device backup so they can come back on a new phone. Nothing else from the game is backed up. Google handles that backup under your account settings; we never receive it.

Buying the game

Exactly $20 is a paid app. You buy it once on Google Play, and Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. There are no in-app purchases: every station and mode is included.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends Google the achievements you unlock, your scores on five leaderboards (Daily Trip, Road Trip, Weekly Challenge, Overtime and Night Shift) and one copy of your save for cloud save. Leaderboards can show your Play Games name and score to other players, depending on your Play Games settings. Google also collects your Play Games profile and some diagnostics; see Google’s Play Games Services data page. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in your Play Games profile at any time. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

What the game doesn’t do

It doesn’t collect your name, email, contacts or location. It has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn’t ask for the internet permission; Play Games goes through Google Play’s own apps on your phone. Tips, prices and Stamps are in-game money only.

Sharing and permissions

Tap Share and Android’s share sheet opens with a picture or a short line about your result. You choose where it goes; the picture stays in the app’s cache until your next share. The only permission is vibrate, for optional haptics you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Exactly $20 is made for players aged 13 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Clearing your data and changes

Erase progress with the reset buttons in Settings, or erase everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling. If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date.
