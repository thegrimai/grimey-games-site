Who we are

Exactly $20 is made by Grimey Games, a studio of GrimAI, Inc. Questions about this policy or your data: hello@grimey.games.

Progress on your device

The game saves your career slots, run and profile stats, achievements, settings and unlock status on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It keeps a copy of this save in the app’s Android preferences.

Backups and new phones

Android may include that preferences copy in your device backup or transfer it to a new phone, depending on your device and Google account settings. You control backups in Android settings.

Buying the full game

The one-time full-game unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; the game never sees your card details. It checks your purchase with Google Play, confirms it, and remembers it on your device so you can play offline. Restoring a purchase checks ownership with Google Play again.

Sharing a result

If you share a result, the game saves the image to its own cache and opens Android’s share sheet. You choose where it goes; the receiving app controls its copy.

Google Play Games

Exactly $20 does not currently use Google Play Games: it doesn’t sign you in, upload scores or achievements, or sync saves through it. Google explains its own processing in its privacy policy.

Clearing your data

Reset career clears your career slots. Reset achievements & Licence clears your profile progress. Clearing the app’s storage removes all local saves and settings; device backups are managed separately in Android, and Google Play keeps its own purchase records. Questions? Email hello@grimey.games.
