Getting started

Your first driver walks you through the pump. In the Practice Lot, hold to pour and let go near the order. Station guides explain each new fuel and rule as you reach it.

Your purchase

Exactly $20 is bought once on Google Play, with everything included. To reinstall it, open Google Play with the account that bought it and find it under your library. For charges and refunds, see Google Play refund help.

Play Games and cloud save

In Settings, choose Sign in to Play Games to earn achievements, post scores to the leaderboards and keep a cloud copy of your save. Signing in is optional; the game plays fully offline without it.

Saves and resets

Settings → About, credits & privacy has the in-game summary. Resetting your career and resetting achievements clear different things, so check before you confirm. The Privacy Policy explains what clearing storage removes.

Reporting a problem

Email hello@grimey.games, or reply through the channel that sent your test build. Tell us the version, the station, your device and what you did. Never send passwords or payment details.
