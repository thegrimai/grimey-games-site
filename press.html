About the studio

Grimey Games is an independent games label of GrimAI, Inc., making games for phones and PC. It is currently developing two very different games: Exactly $20, a pixel-art precision arcade game about pouring fuel to the exact cent, and Realms of Yore, a painted fantasy kingdom simulation where you rule and your people decide.

Exactly $20

Exactly $20

The last cent is the hard part.

Every customer names a price. Hold the pump, ease off, and let go on the exact cent. Start on the night shift at Gas-N-Go and work your way up a 21-station road where dragons breathe on the gauge, control towers call holds mid-pour and the casino hides the price.

  • 21 stations, each with its own fuel, rules and boss
  • A career of stars, promotions, Garage upgrades and Licence levels
  • 29 regulars whose stories unfold over repeat visits
  • Road Trip, Daily Trip, Endless Night Shift and Weekly Challenge modes
  • Overtime and New Game+ after the finale

Realms of Yore

Realms of Yore

You rule. They decide.

Found a settlement in a seeded fantasy world and rule it from the hall. You never pick up a hammer: you set wages, fund institutions, grant charters and post bounties, and your people decide whose work is worth taking. Humans, Dwarves, Orcs and Vampires each rule in their own way.

  • Four playable peoples and eighteen subfactions planned for the alpha
  • Residents who work, save, raise families and inherit on their own
  • Bounties, grants and charters instead of direct orders
  • A painted world, offline and single-player
