About the studio

Grimey Games is the independent games label of GrimAI, Inc. The studio makes Exactly $20, a precision arcade game about pumping fuel, Grimea, a dark fantasy RPG, and Realms of Yore, a painted fantasy kingdom simulation.

Exactly $20

Congratulations, you pump gas for dragons now.

Every customer names a price. Hold the pump, ease off, and let go on the exact cent. Start on the night shift at Gas-N-Go and work your way up a 21-station road where dragons breathe on the gauge, control towers call holds mid-pour and the casino hides the price.

  • 21 stations, each with its own fuel, rules and boss
  • A career of stars, promotions, Garage upgrades and Licence levels
  • 29 regulars whose stories unfold over repeat visits
  • Road Trip, Daily Trip, Endless Night Shift and Weekly Challenge modes
  • Overtime and New Game+ after the finale

Grimea

By the time they meet, you’ll have lived all four lives.

Grimea is a dark fantasy RPG told through four playable lives. Players choose the order of the Human, Elf, Dwarf and Myr stories, building a party through written rooms and turn-based battles. In the full campaign, their paths converge and the four capitals open up in new ways. The public Windows alpha covers the Human opening in Fae Village.

  • Search the village room by room and follow clues in the exploration log.
  • Recruit allies, equip each party member and choose their skills in combat.
  • The planned campaign has four openings, one each for Humans, Elves, Dwarves and Myr, that players can follow in any order.

The Samurai class screen comes from the public alpha’s itch.io page. The other images below show the build in development. The alpha uses AI-assisted placeholder art that the studio plans to replace.

Realms of Yore

Build a kingdom that can tell you no.

Realms of Yore is an offline kingdom simulation about ruling people with lives of their own. A ruler sets wages, grants charters and posts bounties. Residents choose their work, raise families and pass on what they own. Campaigns span rival powers, treaties, sieges and expeditions across the Fray, with earlier realms still under the player’s care.

  • Four playable peoples and eighteen subfactions planned for the alpha
  • Residents who work, save, raise families and inherit on their own
  • Bounties, grants and charters instead of direct orders
  • A painted world, offline and single-player
